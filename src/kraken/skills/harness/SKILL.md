---
name: harness
description: Build coherent guides, sensors and correction loops.
---
Combine feedforward guides with feedback sensors. Prefer fast computational
controls for deterministic rules; reserve inferential review for semantic judgement.
Separate behaviour, maintainability and architecture fitness. Run cheap checks early
and repeat relevant checks after integration. Propose recurring health checks for
gradual drift without launching a background service automatically. Repeated
failures should produce a proposal to improve the harness; never silently relax
checks or permissions. Preserve independent evidence and approved fixtures. The
human approves intent and meaningful trade-offs; sensors do not replace that role.
